# SafeHour Product

## Core problem

A person arriving in an unfamiliar city often does not know how neighborhoods differ, how safety patterns change by time, or which route is preferable at a given moment.

SafeHour translates public safety data into understandable city intelligence.

## Core model

**Time × Place**

Future personalization:

**Time × Place × Person × Route**

## Product principles

1. The map works immediately.
2. Personalization is optional.
3. No mandatory questionnaire.
4. No mandatory ratings.
5. Objective data is separated from personal interpretation.
6. Avoid sensational language.
7. Show confidence and freshness.
8. Never imply that historical patterns predict an individual crime.
9. Do not identify alleged offenders from statistical anomalies.
10. Minimize personal data collection.

## Modes

### Explore
Understand a city and its neighborhoods.

### Tourist / Relocation
Compare neighborhoods before choosing where to stay or live.

### Safety-aware navigation
Choose between fastest, balanced and more cautious routing.

### Alerts
Surface recent serious official incidents when data quality permits.

## Safety Baseline

Users may optionally provide a small baseline through a questionnaire or lightweight feedback.

The baseline changes interpretation and route preference; it does not rewrite objective incident data.

## Anomaly detection

SafeHour may detect unusual spatial-temporal clusters in official incident data. User-facing language should be cautious, for example:

> Unusual incident activity detected

It should not claim that a serial offender or specific person has been identified.

## Accommodation

SafeHour recommends neighborhoods rather than aggregating apartment listings. It can link to popular accommodation services where appropriate.

## Monetization

The consumer product can remain free or low-friction. Longer-term monetization can come from:

- API access
- B2B safety intelligence
- white-label products
- travel and hospitality
- mobility
- insurance
- real-estate analytics
- municipal analytics
